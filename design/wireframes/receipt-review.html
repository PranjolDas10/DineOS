Wireframe · signature screen · for the 15-minute owner test

Receipt review

The owner snaps a supplier receipt; Claude reads it; nothing becomes a price until the owner has decided every line. Lines the model is unsure about come first. Tap through the phone below as the owner would. The numbers are mock data.

Timed task

"Check this receipt from Restaurant Depot and save it."

Hand the phone over and say only that. Don't explain the screen. The stopwatch starts when the receipt opens and stops on "Confirm receipt".

0:00
Watch for
  • Does he find the three "Check this" lines without help?
  • Does he trust "Accept 5 that look right", or open each one?
  • Is "1 sack = 50 lb" a natural way for him to say pack size?
  • Does he notice the ghee line was smudged and fix the name?
  • Where does he hesitate for more than 3 seconds? Write the screen down.
Ask after the task
  1. Do you think about supplier prices per pound, per kilo, or per pack?
  2. Would you want the app to remember "a Restaurant Depot onion sack is 50 lb" next time?
  3. Is the whole photo enough, or do you need the line highlighted on the photo? (Highlighting needs the model to return positions, which costs extra.)
  4. Would you rather review at night on the laptop or right away on the phone?
